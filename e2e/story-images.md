# StoryVerse image browser acceptance checklist

1. Create or open an original user-created world.
2. Confirm the world cover and each chapter beat reserve a stable visual area while loading.
3. With a provider key, confirm generated images match the saved world and beat context.
4. Select a character perspective and confirm the image switches to the selected perspective’s saved beat.
5. Return to a previous chapter and confirm cached images load without a second generation request.
6. Simulate a provider failure or remove the key; confirm labelled fallback art keeps the reader usable and retry remains keyboard accessible.
7. At 390px width, confirm no horizontal overflow and image captions/status remain readable.
